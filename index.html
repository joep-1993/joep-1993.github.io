Centjes

A private keyword research tool for self-published books.

What it does

Centjes is a small command-line tool used by a single independent author. It retrieves average monthly search volumes and competition levels for book-related keywords (for example “mandala coloring book”) from the author's own Google Ads account, using the Google Ads API Keyword Planning services.

How it works

  • The owner signs in once with Google (OAuth) and grants read access to their own Google Ads account.
  • The tool requests historical keyword metrics and keyword ideas for a list of keywords.
  • Results are shown in the terminal and optionally saved to a local CSV file.
  • The tool does not create, change or manage campaigns, ads or budgets.

Who can use it

Centjes is not a public service. It is used only by its owner and accesses only Google Ads accounts the owner manages.
