Privacy Policy

Centjes · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Centjes is a private tool used solely by its owner to retrieve keyword search volume data from the owner's own Google Ads account via the Google Ads API.

Data accessed

Keyword planning data (average monthly search volumes, monthly trends and competition levels) from the owner's Google Ads account. No personal data and no data belonging to other users is accessed.

Use of Google user data

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to display keyword metrics to the owner. Centjes' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. Keyword data is stored only in local files on that computer. Nothing is stored on external servers.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to or transferred to third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
